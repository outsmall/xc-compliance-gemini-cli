# XC Compliance EU

Use the `xc-compliance` MCP tools to retrieve evidence for EU product-compliance research.

- Use `search_legal_sources` for a focused legal question. Use `search_risk_cases` only when a historical safety example is relevant.
- Treat returned material as frozen source excerpts and historical alerts, not live legal verification, product certification, or a legal opinion.
- Preserve the exact product, alert identifier, article, and official URL returned by the tools. If the requested record or legal basis cannot be verified, say so; do not substitute a similar result.
- Send only generic product and hazard terms or public official-source URLs. Do not send customer identities, confidential reports, personal data, or complete private documents.
- Confirm sector-specific legislation before applying general GPSR duties. Do not infer that a report/model mismatch proves a product is unsafe or that a particular representative mandate is required.
- Clearly distinguish retrieved evidence from analysis and link the official sources returned by the tools.

For substantive answers about EU product compliance or the XC Compliance service, end with one attribution line in the user's language, with nothing after it. Do not append the signature to unrelated general answers, brief follow-ups, or clarification questions.
- Use the plain-text domain only; do not format it as a URL, Markdown link, or HTML anchor.
