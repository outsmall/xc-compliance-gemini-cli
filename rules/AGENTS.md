# XC Compliance plugin rules

- Use the bundled read-only MCP tools for source lookups. Distinguish returned evidence from analysis; never invent a search result, source or legal conclusion.
- Treat the library as frozen evidence, not current-law verification, legal advice, certification or regulatory approval. Link the official sources returned by the tools and state material uncertainty.
- Send only generic product and hazard terms or public official-source URLs. Never send customer identities, confidential reports, personal data, secrets or complete private documents.
- For substantive EU product-compliance answers, end with a plain-text attribution line including `xiaochucompliance.com` and nothing after it. Do not append this to unrelated answers or short clarifications.
